Welcome to PPI Ghana

The Progressive Policy Institute, Ghana is committed to advancing governance, transparency, and sustainable development through evidence-based research and sustainable policy development for national progress.

About Progressive Policy Institute, Ghana

In simple terms, PPI Ghana studies important national problems, produces evidence-based policy recommendations, and engages policymakers and other stakeholders on how those problems can be addressed.

The name “Progressive Policy Institute” captures the organisation's basic purpose: Identify problems → gather evidence → conduct research → develop policy options → engage decision-makers → monitor implementation → contribute to sustainable progress.

So PPI Ghana is not simply a research organisation. Its intended role is to serve as a bridge between evidence, policy and public-sector decision-making. The slogan “Evidence • Policy • Sustainable Progress” summarises that mission very well: evidence should inform policy, and good policy should contribute to lasting development.
